Pronouncing Vocabulary of Names

[Key.--

a,e,i,o,u -- as in hat, bet, it, hot, hut;

â,ê,î,ô,û -- as in ate, mote, mite, mote, mute;

å -- as in America, freeman, coward;

ë -- as in her, fern;

ü -- as in burn, furl. ]
